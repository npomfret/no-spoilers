# Christmas Quiz Questions

Editorial question bank. Subject matter: 2000 onward, inclusive.

Use four-option multiple-choice questions with one correct answer. Aim for fun,
little-known trivia, oddities, outliers and surprising statistics. Each answer should
come with an entertaining explanation and sources.

Record direct source links and a checked date for every question, including the evidence
behind any statistic. Current-season drafts below use the 2026 season through Las Vegas;
they do not assume Qatar or Abu Dhabi has happened.

## Question 1: Expensive extras

**Status:** Approved by the user.
**Year:** 2004.

At Monaco in 2004, Jaguar decided its cars’ noses needed which unusual accessory?

- **A.** A piece of Moon rock
- **B.** A diamond
- **C.** A solid-gold lucky coin
- **D.** A meteorite

**Correct answer: B — A diamond.**

The promotion tied in with *Ocean’s Twelve*. After Christian Klien crashed, Jaguar
reported that his car returned without its diamond. A heist-film promotion became
a missing-jewel mystery.

**Sources checked:** 2026-11-23.

- [ABC News — Diamonds not forever for Jaguar](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2004-05-24/diamonds-not-forever-for-jaguar/1981124),
  published 24 May 2004; contemporary reporting includes Jaguar's statement.
- [Formula 1 — Special Monaco Grand Prix liveries](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/superman-star-wars-and-the-missing-diamond-5-special-monaco-grand-prix.7pcegm7Lmuehn5sfoIHKR7),
  published 17 May 2021; retrospective account of the promotion.

**Fact-check note:** The question asks what was attached to the cars. The explanation
describes Jaguar's report at the time; it does not assert how the jewel disappeared
or what ultimately happened to it.

## Question 2: Six laps of fame

**Status:** Approved by the user.
**Year:** 2007.

In his only F1 race, Markus Winkelhock qualified last and led more laps than the
eventual winner. Which team was he driving for at the 2007 European Grand Prix?

- **A.** Super Aguri
- **B.** Toro Rosso
- **C.** Spyker
- **D.** Williams

**Correct answer: C — Spyker.**

Markus Winkelhock qualified last, gambled on wet tyres before the start, and found
himself leading as the rain hit. He was credited with six laps in front; eventual
winner Fernando Alonso led five. It was Winkelhock's only F1 race, giving him a
100% record for leading races. Sample size: one.

**Sources checked:** 2026-11-23.

- [STATS F1 — Europe 2007: Laps led](https://www.statsf1.com/en/2007/europe/en-tete.aspx):

  confirms Alonso as the winner.
- [Motor Sport — Markus Winkelhock: The day I led the European GP in a Spyker](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/markus-winkelhock-the-day-i-led-the-european-gp-in-a-spyker/),
  published 22 July 2020; interview with Winkelhock confirms his sole race, last-place
  qualifying and the tyre gamble.
- [Formula 1 — Father-son duos: The Winkelhocks](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/from-world-champions-to-a-unique-f1-record-holder-16-father-son-duos-in-formula-1.2dlsyoNxXRlJj2XCNYevwP),

**Fact-check note:** Use “qualified last,” because he changed tyres at the end of
the formation lap and started from the pit lane. Leading the restart is not an
official pole position. Avoid a career-laps percentage: sources use different lap
totals around the interrupted race. The race-level statistic is simply 1/1 = 100%.

## Question 3: Rainbow Road, Nevada

**Status:** Draft for user review.
**Year:** 2026 — Las Vegas Grand Prix.

Vegas was so chaotic that Max Verstappen compared the race to which video game?

- **A.** Gran Turismo
- **B.** Need for Speed
- **C.** Mario Kart
- **D.** F1 26

**Correct answer: C — Mario Kart.**

Asked when he had last won a race like that, Verstappen reached for a video-game
comparison: Mario Kart, where dropping back can help you catch up. Apparently the
Las Vegas Strip had become Rainbow Road. The Rainbow Road line is our joke, not his quote.

**Sources checked:** 2026-11-23.

- [FIA — 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix post-race press conference transcript](https://api.fia.com/news/f1-2026-las-vegas-grand-prix-post-race-press-conference-transcript),
  dated 21 November 2026; the opening question under “PRESS CONFERENCE” and Verstappen's
  answer explicitly name Mario Kart and describe the catch-up effect.

## Question 4: The leader who kept getting overtaken

**Status:** Draft for user review.
**Year:** 2026 — Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix had 35 reported lead changes. Yet Max Verstappen was
first at the end of how many of its 50 laps?

- **A.** 15
- **B.** 25
- **C.** 35
- **D.** 45

**Correct answer: D — 45.**

He was first at 90% of the lap endings, despite the frantic fight for the lead.
End-of-lap statistics do not capture every pass and repass within a lap. A tidy
table can hide a thoroughly untidy race.

**Sources checked:** 2026-11-23.

  lap-end leadership totals and ranges.
- [STATS F1 — Las Vegas 2026: Lap by lap](https://www.statsf1.com/en/2026/las-vegas/tour-par-tour.aspx):
  leader sequence used to check the summary.
- [Formula 1 — Las Vegas Lowdown](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/las-vegas-lowdown-all-the-key-moments-as-verstappen-wins-remarkable-race-and-ferraris-drivers-collide-again.62D9SeuQXSboAspMm7N0pN),
  published 22 November 2026; reports 35 lead changes across 50 laps.
- [FIA — Las Vegas post-race press conference](https://api.fia.com/news/f1-2026-las-vegas-grand-prix-post-race-press-conference-transcript):
  corroborates the reported 35 changes in the question to Verstappen.

**Fact-check note:** The 35 reported changes and the lap-end leader table are different
measures. Do not describe 35 as the number of lap-end changes or as 35 verified on-track
overtakes. Recomputing the inclusive ranges gives 45 + 4 + 1 = 50; 45/50 = 90%.

## Question 5: Feeling old yet?

**Status:** Draft for user review.
**Year:** 2026, with a connection to 2006.

Kimi Antonelli, the 2026 world champion, was born in 2006. Which driver still on the
2026 grid won his second world title that very year?

- **A.** Lewis Hamilton
- **B.** Fernando Alonso
- **C.** Max Verstappen
- **D.** Lando Norris

**Correct answer: B — Fernando Alonso.**

The year Antonelli arrived, Alonso collected title number two. Twenty years later,
they shared the grid.

**Sources checked:** 2026-11-23.

- [Formula 1 — The records Antonelli broke on the way to his maiden title](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/in-numbers-the-records-antonelli-has-broken-on-his-way-to-his-maiden-f1-title.2FB9yQ5qD7XzQ418HNlrfN),
  published 2 November 2026; the section “First driver born in the 2000s to become World
  Champion” explicitly connects Antonelli's 2006 birth with active driver Alonso's
  second championship that year.

**Fact-check note:** The connection is the calendar year. Do not suggest Alonso had
already won his second title on the day Antonelli was born.
