# Christmas Quiz Questions

Editorial question bank. Subject matter: 2000 onward, inclusive.

Use four-option multiple-choice questions with one correct answer. Aim for fun,
little-known trivia, oddities, outliers and surprising statistics. Each answer should
come with an entertaining explanation and sources.

Selected questions may have an optional clue; add one where it helps make the
question fun and approachable. A correct answer earns **1 point unaided**, or
**½ point after taking a clue**; an incorrect answer earns 0. Offer clues on
request before the answer is submitted, stating the reduced score before revealing
the clue. Questions without a clue remain worth 1 point for a correct answer.

Record direct source links and a checked date for every question, including the evidence
behind any statistic. Current-season drafts below use the 2026 season through Las Vegas;
they do not assume Qatar or Abu Dhabi has happened.

## Question 1: Expensive extras

**Status:** Approved by the user.
**Year:** 2004.

At Monaco in 2004, Jaguar decided its cars’ noses needed which unusual accessory?

- **A.** A piece of Moon rock
- **B.** A diamond
- **C.** A solid-gold lucky coin
- **D.** A meteorite

**Correct answer: B — A diamond.**

The promotion tied in with *Ocean’s Twelve*. After Christian Klien crashed, Jaguar
reported that his car returned without its diamond. A heist-film promotion became
a missing-jewel mystery.

**Sources checked:** 2026-11-23.

- [ABC News — Diamonds not forever for Jaguar](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2004-05-24/diamonds-not-forever-for-jaguar/1981124),
  published 24 May 2004; contemporary reporting includes Jaguar's statement.

  published 17 May 2021; retrospective account of the promotion.

**Fact-check note:** The question asks what was attached to the cars. The explanation
describes Jaguar's report at the time; it does not assert how the jewel disappeared

## Question 2: Six laps of fame

**Status:** Approved by the user.
**Year:** 2007.

In his only F1 race, Markus Winkelhock qualified last and led more laps than the
eventual winner. Which team was he driving for at the 2007 European Grand Prix?

- **A.** Super Aguri
- **B.** Toro Rosso
- **C.** Spyker
- **D.** Williams

**Correct answer: C — Spyker.**

Markus Winkelhock qualified last, gambled on wet tyres before the start, and found
himself leading as the rain hit. He was credited with six laps in front; eventual
winner Fernando Alonso led five. It was Winkelhock's only F1 race, giving him a
100% record for leading races. Sample size: one.

## Question 4: The leader who kept getting overtaken

**Status:** Draft for user review.
**Year:** 2026 — Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix had 35 reported lead changes. Yet Max Verstappen was
first at the end of how many of its 50 laps?

- **A.** 15

- **C.** 35

**Correct answer: D — 45.**

He was first at 90% of the lap endings, despite the frantic fight for the lead.

## Question 5: Feeling old yet?

## Question 6: Four of a kind

**Status:** Rejected by the user as confusing; retained as research, not part of the quiz.
**Period:** 2000–2025 inclusive.

Between 2000 and 2025, which of these countries produced as many different F1
Grand Prix winners as Germany?

- **A.** France
- **B.** Finland
- **C.** Brazil
- **D.** Netherlands

**Correct answer: B — Finland.**

Four Finns won during that period: Mika Häkkinen, Kimi Räikkönen, Heikki Kovalainen
and Valtteri Bottas. Germany's four were Michael Schumacher, Ralf Schumacher,
Sebastian Vettel and Nico Rosberg. The Schumachers, Vettel and Rosberg sound like
an intimidating collection — but Finland matched the headcount.

## Question 11: An early finish

last won a Grand Prix that season?

- **A.** June
- **B.** August

- **D.** October

**Correct answer: A — June.**

Button won in Turkey on 7 June, then did not win any of the season's remaining
