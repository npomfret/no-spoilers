No Spoilers is a Home Screen widget. That is the product on iPhone and iPad.

Please add the widget before reviewing. A widget is not visible until someone places it, so a review that only launches the app does not see what the app is for.

TO ADD IT
1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
2. Tap the widget button at the top of the screen (Edit, then Add Widget).
3. Search for "No Spoilers" and pick a size.

There are three sizes and each shows a different amount of the race weekend:
- Small: the Grand Prix, the next session, and a live countdown to it.
- Medium: the next session with its countdown, the last session that finished, and the weekend after this one.
- Large: every session of the weekend with its status, plus what is coming next.

WHAT IT DOES
It shows when the sessions of a Grand Prix weekend are, which of them have already finished and are therefore safe to watch on replay, and a live countdown to the next one.

It never shows a result. This is not a setting: there are no result fields anywhere in the app's data model, so there is nothing to hide, collapse, or fetch on demand.

The schedule is cached on the device in a shared App Group container, which the widget reads directly. The widget and its countdown keep working with no network connection.

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED
There is no sign-in anywhere in the app, so the demo account previously attached to this submission does not apply to anything. All functionality is available immediately on launch.
