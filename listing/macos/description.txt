No Spoilers lives in your menu bar and keeps you on top of the race weekend — without ruining it.

Every session of the weekend is listed: Practice, Qualifying, Sprint, and Race. Sessions that have finished are marked as safe to watch. Upcoming sessions show a live countdown. The result never appears.

FEATURES
- Menu bar access — always one click away
- The whole Grand Prix weekend at a glance
- A safe-to-watch marker on every session that has finished, using the time it actually ended
- Live countdown to the next session
- Keeps itself up to date with the published calendar

Whether you are watching live or catching up later, No Spoilers tells you exactly what you can watch — and nothing more.
